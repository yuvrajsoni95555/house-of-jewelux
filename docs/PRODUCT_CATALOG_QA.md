# Product Catalogue QA — House of Jewelux

## Current catalogue snapshot

- 50 product records.
- 5 categories: 10 rings, 10 earrings, 10 bracelets, 10 necklaces and 10 brooches.
- 22 unique primary image paths are used across the 50 records.
- 16 primary image paths are reused by multiple products.
- 45 records use generic category-reference photography rather than a product-specific verified image.
- 5 records are marked as client-supplied reference photography in the existing metadata.

## Important image limitation

The current catalogue reuses the same small set of category images across many different SKUs. Assigning a different image to each SKU by guessing would risk showing customers the wrong jewellery design. This repair pass therefore does not fabricate or randomly reassign product photos.

Before the catalogue is treated as launch-ready, upload and map one verified front-facing product photo (and optional detail/side/back views) to each SKU. Product photos should show the exact piece being sold.

## Product data safeguards applied in this repair

- Removed generic BIS hallmark wording from the shared metal field.
- Removed generic certificate-card text that was repeated across every SKU.
- Removed unverified gemstone "Certified" wording from the stone summary.
- Removed placeholder ratings and review counts from the product data.
- Marked generic reference photography as pending product-specific verification.
- Removed the uniform 26% compare-at discount from every SKU because no verified original-price evidence was available.

## Required owner input

Provide the final product-specific:
1. Main image and gallery images.
2. Exact base metal and plating/finish.
3. Stone type and whether natural, lab-grown or simulant.
4. Hallmark/certificate details only where a real certificate or hallmark is available.
5. Weight, dimensions and size options.
6. Final selling price and a genuine reference/MRP price only where it is documented.

Until these details are verified, do not add BIS, diamond certification, gemstone origin, hypoallergenic, water-safe, anti-tarnish lifetime, or warranty claims to product pages.

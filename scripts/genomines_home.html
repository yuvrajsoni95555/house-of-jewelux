We Grow Metal

Biological infrastructure for critical metal extraction. Battery-grade nickel, harvested at best-in-class costs.

Harvesting metal

Inside the team replacing the excavator with plants, and the biotech built to power the next century of critical metal extraction.

Our paradigm shift

Genomines cultivates “metal farms” to reinforce the world’s most critical metal supply chains. Our plants extract nickel from low-grade, underutilized soils and transform idle lands into high-yield, profitable assets.

The tech

At scale.

Low-cost agriculture as the future of metal production. Our metal farms produce nickel at first-quartile cash costs and with significantly less capex.

Systemic Return.

Every ton of nickel we produce compounds value across three dimensions: financial return, stable supply chains, and planetary health.

Return on Investment
-40%

Lower cash cost per ton versus traditional extraction. Legacy operators require fleets of heavy extractors, crushers, and grinders to produce the same outcome that our plants achieve naturally. 

Return on Stability
70 Million +

Tons of potential annual nickel supply unlocked by Genomines' technology. Newly accessible reserves reinforce critical supply chains by diversifying nickel production away from single points of failure.

Return on Environment
- 45 tons

CO2 equivalent per ton of nickel vs. traditional laterite extraction. Simple and low-input extraction processes reduce our reliance on fossil fuels and rehabilitate the soil.

Our Craft

The Jewelux Finish

Why our silver does not look like everyone else's silver.

Most silver jewellery is polished once and shipped. Ours passes through multiple rounds of hand-burnishing and micron gold dip in our Jaipur atelier, achieving a warm, heirloom depth that feels timeless.

Solid 925 sterling silver is sealed with anti-tarnish rhodium or liquid 18k gold tone, giving it the presence, weight, and quiet majesty of ceremonial gold.

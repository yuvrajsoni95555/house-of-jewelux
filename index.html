Our Origins & Heritage

House of Jewelux

"Where Elegance Becomes Eternal"

Founded by Yuvraj Soni, HOUSE OF JEWELUX was born with a vision to create fine jewelry that celebrates the beauty of Indian heritage while embracing modern luxury and international design inspiration.

Indian Heritage × Global Sophistication

Our jewelry designs are created in India and are deeply inspired by Indian culture, traditions, heritage, artistry, architecture, and the timeless significance of jewelry in Indian life. Every piece is brought to life by skilled master artisans, honoring centuries of ancestral goldsmith techniques with deliberate, contemplative fabrication.

Alongside our Indian cultural foundation, some of our designs take inspiration from Milan, Italy — particularly its fashion, architecture, contemporary elegance, and sophisticated design language. We bring these influences together to create jewelry that feels distinctly Indian while maintaining a refined, modern, and globally appealing aesthetic.

At HOUSE OF JEWELUX, we believe jewelry should be more than an accessory. It should represent individuality, meaningful moments, personal identity, and timeless elegance — crafted to be cherished for generations.

✦ FROM INSPIRATION TO LEGACY

FROM AN IDEA TO A LEGACY

“Every House of Jewelux creation begins long before silver touches the mould. It begins with inspiration — drawn from our heritage, nature, culture, architecture, artistry and the world around us.”

“We transform that inspiration into a design, the design into a creation, and the creation into a piece made to become part of a legacy.”

01 — INSPIRATION 01

WHERE EVERY CREATION BEGINS

“Before we draw a single line, we look for inspiration. Our designs may emerge from Indian heritage, traditional craftsmanship, nature and wildlife, architecture, culture, history, or the beauty of everyday life.”

“We do not begin with a jewellery piece. We begin with a story.”

02 — DESIGN 02

FROM INSPIRATION TO FORM

“Once the inspiration is defined, our design team translates the idea into detailed drawings. Proportion, silhouette, stone placement, balance and wearability are carefully considered before the design moves forward.”

“Every line has a purpose.”

03 — 3D DEVELOPMENT 03

THE DESIGN TAKES SHAPE

“Our team transforms the drawing into a precise 3D jewellery model. This allows us to refine proportions, dimensions, structure, stone positions and overall form before creating the physical piece.”

“From a drawing on paper to a form that can be seen from every angle.”

04 — WAX PROTOTYPE 04

FROM DIGITAL FORM TO PHYSICAL FORM

“Once the 3D design is approved, a precise wax or casting prototype is created. This physical form allows our team to inspect the design, proportions and construction before casting the final silver piece.”

“A micron-accurate CPX wax prototype bridges the digital blueprint into tangible reality.”

05 — CASTING 05

THE DESIGN BECOMES SILVER

“The wax pattern is prepared for casting. Molten silver is carefully introduced into the mould, replacing the wax form and creating the initial silver jewellery piece.”

“This is the moment when the design leaves the drawing board and becomes a physical creation.”

06 — HAND FINISHING 06

REFINED BY HAND

“After casting, the silver piece is carefully inspected and hand-finished. Excess metal, casting marks and imperfections are removed, while edges, surfaces and details are refined to achieve the intended form.”

“Every surface is touched, refined and prepared by skilled hands.”

07 — STONE SETTING 07

THE STONES FIND THEIR PLACE

“Each stone is carefully selected according to the design, size, colour, character and intended aesthetic of the piece. Our artisans then set the stones into their precise positions.”

“Diamonds, gemstones and other selected stones are individually positioned and secured with precision.”

08 — POLISHING 08

BRINGING THE SILVER TO LIFE

“Once the stones are securely set, the jewellery undergoes careful polishing. Surfaces are refined to create the desired level of smoothness, brilliance and finish while preserving the details of the original design.”

“Transforming raw silver into a luminous, mirror-polished heirloom.”

09 — SIGNATURE FINISH 09

THE FINAL SURFACE

“Depending on the design, selected pieces receive a refined rhodium finish or micron gold plating. These finishes are chosen to achieve the intended colour, appearance and character of the jewellery.”

Rhodium Finish

“Bright, refined and contemporary.”

Micron Gold Finish

“Warm, rich and luxurious.”

“Tailored individually to the unique soul of each design.”

10 — FINAL INSPECTION 10

EVERY DETAIL, REVIEWED

“Before a creation leaves our atelier, it is carefully inspected for finishing, stone security, surface quality, proportions and overall presentation.”

“Only after the final details are reviewed is the jewellery prepared for its new owner.”

Shipping & White-Glove Delivery Protocol

Armored Transit & Complete Transit Insurance

Every acquisition from HOUSE OF JEWELUX is handled with sovereign discretion. All shipments are packed in tamper-evident, unmarked vault containers and entrusted exclusively to armored couriers (Brinks or Malca-Amit).

Complimentary Global Insured Delivery

We offer complimentary insured delivery worldwide for all orders over ₹8,00,000 INR (or equivalent currency). Orders below this threshold incur a flat courier fee of ₹12,500.

Delivery Timelines

  • In-Stock Masterpieces: Dispatched within 48 business hours with 2-3 business day worldwide transit.
  • Sizing Adjustments: Adds 3-5 business days for master goldsmith sizing.
  • Bespoke Commissions: Typically 4-6 weeks from design sign-off.

Customs & Duties

For international shipments, all duties, taxes, and clearance fees are calculated and collected during checkout or handled seamlessly by our private concierge.

Returns & Refunds Guarantee

14-Day Sovereign Peace of Mind

We want you to cherish your piece unconditionally. If for any reason your catalogue acquisition does not meet your expectations, we provide a 14-day complimentary return or exchange period.

Return Conditions

  • The piece must be in unworn, pristine condition with security seal tags intact.
  • All original GIA, Gübelin, or SSEF certificates, warranty cards, and bespoke caskets must be included.
  • Custom bespoke commissions (personalized engraved or custom-sized pieces) are non-refundable, but include unlimited complimentary adjustments.

How to Initiate a Return

Contact concierge@houseofjewelux.com or your dedicated private client advisor. We will dispatch an armored courier to collect the package directly from your residence or office at no charge to you.

Privacy Policy & Discretion Covenant

Bank-Grade Client Data Confidentiality

HOUSE OF JEWELUX operates under stringent Swiss Federal Data Protection Act (FADP) and EU GDPR regulations. We treat our clients’ identities with absolute discretion.

We never disclose, rent, or sell client information to third parties. All financial transactions are processed through 256-bit encrypted bank networks with zero cardholder data retained on our servers.

Styling Guide · 5 Min Read

Styling Jewellery With a Saree, Without Overloading

By The Jewelux Atelier

A heavy Kanjeevaram or Banarasi weave carries immense visual weight on its own. When paired with oversized necklaces, massive jhumkas, and multiple cuffs simultaneously, the silhouette quickly loses its focal poetry.

The Rule of One Hero Piece

Choose one solitary star for the evening. If your neckline carries our Meharbaan Chandelier Jhumkas, leave the collar bare or wear a whisper-thin Saanjh layering chain. If wearing our Rani Ceremonial Collar, let the ears carry discreet Zarina halo studs.

The Philosophy

Where Milanese Couture Meets Four Generations of Goldsmith Lineage

HOUSE OF JEWELUX was founded on a singular conviction: fine jewellery must transcend fast-fashion consumerism to become a permanent talisman of love, identity, and personal triumph.

In our design studio in Milan, our creative directors draw inspiration from Didone typography, architectural chiaroscuro, and the natural posture of the modern woman. We strip ornamentation of needless clutter, pursuing pure sculptural balance.

In our workshops in Geneva and India, master artisans whose ancestral lineages span four generations translate these blueprints into reality. Solid 925 sterling silver is smelted with anti-tarnish rhodium shields, while conflict-free lab moissanites and diamonds are hand-seated with optical micrometers.

The Modern Silver Focus

While traditional jewellery houses inflate retail margins through heavy gold markups, HOUSE OF JEWELUX brings high-jewellery grade stone-setting and platinum-grade finishing to accessible, hallmarked 925 sterling silver—empowering modern women and newly married couples to celebrate daily luxury without compromise.

Guaranteed Integrity

The Three Atelier Covenants

Every creation bearing the House of Jewelux seal adheres to unyielding standards of provenance, purity, and longevity.

💎

100% Conflict-Free & Certified

Every gemstone complies strictly with the Kimberley Process. Solitaires above 1.0 carat include GIA or GRA laboratory dossiers verifying optical fire, clarity, and cut geometry.

🛡️

925 Stamped & Anti-Tarnish

Our solid sterling silver pieces receive a multi-layer liquid rhodium dip, preserving mirror brightness and shielding against oxidation, daily water contact, and skin sensitivity.

📜

Lifetime Atelier Covenant

Every client enjoys lifetime complimentary prong tightening, ultrasonic cleansing, and rhodium re-dipping across our Genevan, Milanese, and Indian salon partners.

Generational Craft

The Five Stages of Haute Joaillerie

How a sketch transforms into an enduring family heirloom.

01
Gouache Sketch

Hand-painted watercolor renderings capturing optical fire and silhouette balance in Milan.

02
Wax Micro-Sculpting

Micron-accurate 3D prototypes calibrated to human ergonomics and comfort-fit finger radii.

03
Noble Metallurgy

Smelting certified 925 sterling silver and 18K champagne gold under inert atmospheric protection.

04
Hand Stone-Setting

Pavé, bezel, and micro-claw settings executed under 40x stereoscopic gemological microscopes.

05
Rhodium Lustre

Triple liquid-rhodium bath sealing against tarnish and final GIA/GRA serial number stamping.
